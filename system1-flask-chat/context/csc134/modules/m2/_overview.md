## Make-gradient position (why M2 sits where it does)

M2 is in the **M2–M4 type-in-100% band**: the Apply beat hands students a
complete, working program to type in verbatim, compile, run, and then
deliberately break. There is no "finish the 80%" scaffolding here and no
spec-only handoff — those arrive later (M5–M7, M8). Do not drift this
position during deep-build.
