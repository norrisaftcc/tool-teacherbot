## Rotate the Role, Keep the Practices

Sprint 2 changes which responsibility you own at depth. It does not excuse you from any core practice you learned in Sprint 1.

### The rotation

Your instructor assigns the new role. On the normal path, you should not hold the same role twice.

Role | Owns at depth

**Product Owner** | the Sprint 2 scope decision, explicit refusals, and traceability to product evidence;

**Developer** | a defined implementation slice and the tests covering that slice;

**Quality Lead** | the standard, regression evidence, and review coordination.

### Do not restart the role from zero

You watched someone else hold this responsibility during Sprint 1. Read the existing evidence before creating Sprint 2 artifacts. The point of rotation is to learn from a new perspective, not to erase the previous one.

Carry-forward first

Read the M6 scope decision, Definition of Done, review findings, and retrospective before making a Sprint 2 decision in your new role.

### Every student still does every core practice

- write/run tests for their own work;
- review someone else's implementation;
- contribute to the increment;
- use source/acceptance evidence to classify mismatches;
- participate in review and retrospective.

**Next:** Sprint 2 introduces a data-layer change. Your old tests become protection against accidentally changing product behavior while the inside of the system changes.
