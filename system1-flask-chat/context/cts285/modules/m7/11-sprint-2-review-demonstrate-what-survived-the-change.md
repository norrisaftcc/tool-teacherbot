## Sprint 2 Review — Demonstrate What Survived the Change

Sprint 2 review is larger than Sprint 1 because the pod can now show not only what was added, but what remained true while the inside of the system changed.

How this is graded

The review and retrospective remain required but do not create a separate presentation grade.

### Demonstrate the evidence chain

Each student accounts for their rotated role, then the pod demonstrates one protected behavior through the change:

1. the acceptance criterion or prior behavior that mattered;
2. the test or observable evidence protecting it before the data-layer change;
3. the change made in Sprint 2;
4. the post-change result;
5. any failure that required investigation and what layer was revised.

### Account for the rotated role

Use the same four questions as Sprint 1: what did the role own, what evidence shows your work, what changed from the plan, and what should the next stage inherit?

### Sprint 2 retrospective

Create `docs/decisions/m7-sprint-retrospective.md`. Record:

- whether the M6 “Try in Sprint 2” action was actually tried and what happened;
- one practice to keep;
- one practice to change before capstone work;
- one technical or product risk that M8 must place in the handoff package;
- one unresolved question the next course must be able to see.

**This review closes product delivery in CTS-285.** Module 8 does not ask for another sprint. It packages what the capstone needs to inherit.

Before leaving Sprint 2, make sure the repository exposes the current application, regression tests, both sprint decisions, both retrospectives, current Definition of Done, review evidence, and unresolved risks/questions.
