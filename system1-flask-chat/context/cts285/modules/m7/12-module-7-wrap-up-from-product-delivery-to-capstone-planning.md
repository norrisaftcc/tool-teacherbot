You have now delivered two sprint increments, held two role perspectives on the normal path, and used executable evidence to protect behavior through change. The final module changes the question from *what should we build next?* to *what must the capstone inherit?*

- design evidence from M4;

- two sprint decisions and retrospectives;

### What Module 8 must create or harden

The capstone handoff still needs a hardened working agreement, a validated handoff view of the backlog and wireframes, a design brief, design-consult evidence, a seeded risk register, an exit checklist, and the product-handoff decision once the cross-course contract settles it.

**Do not rebuild good evidence just because the module changed.** M8 is an audit-and-handoff module. Existing artifacts should be validated and hardened, not copied into new files for the sake of another submission.
