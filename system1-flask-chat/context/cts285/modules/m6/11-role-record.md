CTS-285 · Module 6 · Graded Evidence

## Role Record

Show the evidence that you performed the Sprint 1 role assigned to you at depth. This is an individual grade. The repository evidence matters more than a claim that the work happened.

Outcome

**MLO 6.3 → CLO 4, CLO 5:** perform a defined sprint responsibility, leave attributable evidence, and account for the decisions or standard owned by that role.

### Use the section for your assigned role

Product Owner

Your primary evidence is `docs/decisions/m6-sprint-scope-decision.md` plus repository evidence showing that the sprint stayed traceable to the selected scope. The record must show what the pod committed to, what it explicitly refused, and what evidence supported the decision.

Developer

Your primary evidence is your own pull request/commits and the tests covering your slice. The record must make the boundary of your contribution visible and show how the implementation remained traceable to the sprint acceptance criteria.

Quality Lead

Your primary evidence is `docs/definition-of-done.md`, the review coordination trail, and the pod’s application of the standard. The record must show that the standard was in place before the result was known and was checkable by someone other than its author.

### Submit one evidence link

Submit the repository location that best anchors your role evidence. Your instructor will follow linked files, commits, or pull requests from there. Do not create a separate reflection document merely to restate what the repository already shows.

**One assignment, one rubric:** everyone is graded with the same 9-point Role Record rubric. Your assigned role changes the evidence the instructor expects to see, not the Canvas assignment or rubric attached to it.

Your role rotates in Module 7. Sprint 2 does not erase this record; it gives you a second perspective on the same delivery process.
