## Sprint 1 Review — Account for Your Role

Sprint 1 closes by comparing what the pod committed to with what actually happened. Each student accounts for their own role evidence; then the pod records what should change in Sprint 2.

How this is graded

The review and retrospective are required, but they do not add a separate grade. Your graded evidence is already in Sprint 1 Contribution, Code Review Given, and Role Record.

### Part 1 — Review the increment

Use the sprint scope decision and Definition of Done as the reference points. The review is not a sales pitch. It is an evidence account.

Each student should be ready to answer these four questions in a brief role account:

1. **What did my role own?**
2. **What evidence shows what I actually did?**
3. **What changed from the original sprint commitment, if anything?**
4. **What should Sprint 2 know because of this sprint?**

**Keep the repository open.** The strongest answer is not a longer explanation. It is a short claim with evidence another person can inspect.

### Part 2 — Apply the Definition of Done

Do not hide a failed line to make the review cleaner. Show the result as recorded: met or not met, with fix now / accept and document / out of scope where needed. A sprint can be useful without being perfect.

### Part 3 — Retrospective

The review asks, *What did we deliver?* The retrospective asks, *How did we work, and what should change next time?* Those are different questions.

As a pod, record a short retrospective in `docs/decisions/m6-sprint-retrospective.md` with:

- **Keep:** one practice that helped the pod produce trustworthy evidence;
- **Change:** one practice that caused delay, confusion, or weak evidence;
- **Try next sprint:** one specific adjustment the pod can actually make in M7;
- **Carry-forward question:** one unresolved product, technical, or source question Sprint 2 must see.

**The retrospective is pod evidence, not a group grade.** It is recorded and read because Sprint 2 should inherit what Sprint 1 learned. Candor is more useful here than scoring.

### The bridge to Sprint 2

Before leaving the review, confirm that the next sprint can find:

- the final Sprint 1 scope decision;
- the current Definition of Done;
- the test suite protecting Sprint 1 behavior;
- the code review evidence;
- the retrospective and carry-forward question.

**Next:** Module 7 rotates roles and reduces scaffolding. The process does not restart; Sprint 2 inherits this evidence and changes the system under greater pressure.
