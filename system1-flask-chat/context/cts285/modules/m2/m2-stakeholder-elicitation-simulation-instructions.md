# M2 Simulation — Stakeholder Elicitation Under Pressure

## Purpose
In this simulation, you will act as the systems analyst for the DataMan modernization project. You will not be given complete information at the start. Your job is to decide what to investigate, separate evidence from assumptions, and make a requirements decision you can defend.

## What You Will Do
1. Review the initial stakeholder statements.
2. Choose only three questions to investigate.
3. Use the evidence you uncover to draft an initial requirements position.
4. Respond to a new project complication.
5. Revise, keep, defer, or reopen a decision based on the new evidence.
6. Generate a Markdown decision record.
7. Add that decision record to your CTS-285 GitHub repository at:
   `docs/decisions/m2-elicitation-decision-record.md`
8. Use the record to update your DataMan Requirements Register.

## Important
This is not a right-or-wrong quiz. Different investigation paths may produce different defensible decisions.

Your work should show that you can:
- identify relevant evidence;
- recognize what is still uncertain;
- avoid treating a proposed solution as a confirmed requirement;
- draft a requirement supported by evidence;
- explain why a decision is defensible; and
- respond professionally when new information changes the project context.

## What to Submit
Do not submit screenshots of the simulation.

Your evidence is the decision record you add to your own GitHub repository and the requirements work that it supports. Follow the Canvas submission directions for the associated DataMan Requirements Register assignment.
