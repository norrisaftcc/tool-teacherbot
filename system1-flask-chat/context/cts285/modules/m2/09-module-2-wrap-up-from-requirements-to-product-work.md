CTS-285 • Module 2 Wrap-Up

## From Requirements to Product Work

You began this module with vague requests and incomplete stakeholder information. You should now have a defensible set of DataMan requirements that can guide the next stage of the project.

Your artifact

**Module 2 focus:** move from stakeholder and system evidence to clear, testable functional and non-functional requirements.

**Your primary project evidence:** `docs/requirements.md`

### What You Should Be Able to Do Now

- distinguish functional requirements, non-functional requirements, ambiguous requests, and proposed solutions;
- ask clarifying questions that uncover the need behind a stakeholder request;
- separate evidence from assumptions and implementation preferences;
- write requirements that are clear, grounded, testable, focused, and solution-neutral enough for this stage of the project;
- revise a requirement when new evidence changes what the team can defend; and
- trace a requirement backward through **Evidence → Need → Requirement**.

### Before You Leave Module 2

Confirm that your DataMan Requirements Register is complete, committed, synced, and submitted in Canvas using the direct GitHub URL to **docs/requirements.md**.

- At least four functional requirements are present.
- At least three non-functional requirements are present.
- Each confirmed requirement includes a source or rationale.
- Open questions and assumptions are separated from confirmed requirements.
- Your simulation decision record is saved in **docs/decisions/** and used where relevant as project evidence.

### The Project Does Not Reset Here

Your Requirements Register is not a one-module document. Module 3 will use these requirements as input for product work. You will begin translating requirements into user stories, acceptance criteria, estimates, and prioritized backlog decisions.

**Evidence → Need → Requirement → User Story → Acceptance Criteria → Priority**

### Carry These Habits Forward

In the next module, continue asking the same professional questions:

- What evidence supports this?
- What are we still assuming?
- Is this the need, or have we jumped to a solution?
- Could another team member understand and verify this?
- What changes when new information appears?

### Module 3 Preview

Next, the focus shifts from **what the system must do** to **how the product team turns those requirements into workable, prioritized product decisions**. Your DataMan requirements become the starting evidence—not something you leave behind.
