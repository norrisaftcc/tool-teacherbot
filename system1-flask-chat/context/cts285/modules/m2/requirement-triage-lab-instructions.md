# Requirement Triage Lab — Student Instructions

## Purpose
Before you classify requirements for the DataMan project, practice the same reasoning in several different software situations. The goal is to recognize what kind of statement you are looking at and what should happen next.

This is a low-stakes practice activity. Use the feedback after each attempt to improve your reasoning before you move into the DataMan-specific work.

## What you will do
In this New Quiz, you will:

1. sort software statements into four categories;
2. identify when a stakeholder statement needs clarification;
3. distinguish a requirement from a proposed technical or interface solution;
4. identify the strongest next step for a systems analyst.

## Categories you will use
- **Functional Requirement** — what the system must do
- **Non-Functional Requirement** — a quality, constraint, or condition on how the system must operate
- **Ambiguous / Needs Clarification** — too vague, incomplete, or not yet testable
- **Proposed Solution** — an implementation or design choice that may or may not address the underlying need

## How to complete the lab
1. Read each statement carefully.
2. Ask whether it describes required behavior, a quality/constraint, an unclear need, or a proposed solution.
3. Complete all items in the quiz.
4. Submit your attempt.
5. Read the feedback for any item you missed.
6. Retry the lab if needed until you can explain why each statement belongs in its category.

## What matters most
Do not memorize wording. Focus on the reasoning behind the classification.

A professional systems analyst should be able to ask:

- What does the system need to do?
- What quality or operating condition matters?
- What still needs to be clarified?
- Is this really a requirement, or has someone already jumped to a solution?
