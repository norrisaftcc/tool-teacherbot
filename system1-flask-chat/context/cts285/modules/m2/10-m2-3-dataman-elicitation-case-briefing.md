## DataMan Elicitation Case

A stakeholder has raised a concern. Your job is to investigate before the team turns that concern into a requirement or a solution.

Outcome

**MLO 2.2** — Apply elicitation techniques to clarify ambiguous stakeholder statements and identify the need behind a request.

### Your Role

You are the systems analyst for the DataMan modernization project. In the simulation, you will choose an elicitation move, follow the evidence it produces, recover if you jump to a solution too early, and use multiple evidence sources to clarify what the system actually needs to do.

Analyst rule

**A concern is evidence that investigation is needed.** It is not automatically a finished requirement.

### As You Work

- Notice what each elicitation technique adds to the evidence trail.
- Keep stakeholder concerns, observed behavior, documented behavior, requirements, and proposed solutions separate.
- Do not assume that an existing feature is defective simply because a learner experiences difficulty.
- Pay attention to what the original DataMan documentation establishes before recommending a design change.

### What the Case Should Leave You With

By the end of the simulation, you should be able to explain how interview evidence, observation, and document analysis together clarify a requirement without prematurely choosing the implementation.

Your artifact

The requirements surfaced in the case are candidates for your **DataMan Requirements Register** at `docs/requirements.md`. Record the evidence source for each requirement when you build the register.
