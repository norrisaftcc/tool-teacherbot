## Module 3 Wrap-Up — From Product Work to Design

You started this module with validated requirements. You are leaving it with product work that a team can understand, test, prioritize, and carry forward.

### What changed in Module 3

You moved from asking **what the replacement system should do** to deciding **what work should move forward, in what order, and why**.

**Validated Requirement → User Story → Acceptance Criteria → Backlog Decision → Priority**

Your artifact

**Your Module 3 evidence.** The work you produced now forms a connected product-decision record:

- `docs/requirements.md` — validated requirements carried forward from Module 2
- `docs/decisions/m3-backlog-triage-record.md` — readiness and triage decisions
- `docs/decisions/m3-product-owner-decision-record.md` — constrained release decisions and tradeoffs
- `docs/product-backlog.md` — your final traceable, testable, prioritized product backlog

### What you should now be able to do

- Turn validated requirements into user-centered product work.
- Write acceptance criteria that make a story observable and testable.
- Recognize when work is too large, uncertain, dependent, or not ready.
- Use value, effort, dependencies, risk, stakeholder need, and capacity to make backlog decisions.
- Revise a plan when new information changes what is feasible or defensible.
- Explain why some work moves forward while other work waits.

Your artifact

**Before you leave Module 3.** Open `docs/product-backlog.md` one final time and confirm that:

- every story traces to a validated requirement;
- acceptance criteria are observable and testable;
- dependencies, constraints, and uncertainty are visible;
- priorities are supported by clear product reasoning; and
- the rendered Markdown is readable and complete.
