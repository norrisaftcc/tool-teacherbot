## M3.4 — Backlog Triage: What Moves Forward?

You have practiced building usable stories and acceptance criteria. Now make product decisions when not every item is equally ready, equally valuable, or equally easy to move forward.

### Your task

Open the interactive triage experience. You will review six backlog items and decide whether each should:

- **Move Forward**
- **Refine**
- **Defer**

Each choice reveals a consequence. After you commit to your first-round decisions, new information will arrive and you will have a chance to revise.

### Use the same decision lenses from M3.3

**Value • Relative effort • Dependencies • Risk / uncertainty • Readiness**

[Open Backlog Triage](https://amlw05.github.io/cts-285-course-simulations/m3/backlog-triage/)

Your artifact

**Before you close the simulation.** Generate the **M3 Backlog Triage Decision Record** and copy it into your CTS-285 GitHub repository at `docs/decisions/m3-backlog-triage-record.md`. You will use that record in the graded Canvas follow-up immediately after this activity.

### What counts here

You are not being rewarded for guessing a hidden perfect backlog. What matters is whether your decisions respond to the available evidence, constraints, and consequences—and whether you revise when the situation changes.
