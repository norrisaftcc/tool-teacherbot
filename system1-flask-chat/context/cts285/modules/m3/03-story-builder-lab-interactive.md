## Story Builder Lab — Find the User, Need, and Value

Instead of answering a quiz, you will build, repair, and split product stories using an interactive lab.

### What you will do

1. Build a story from a validated requirement by choosing the user, capability, and value.
2. Repair a weak story that centers implementation instead of user value.
3. Decide when a story has become too broad and should be split.
4. Transfer the same reasoning to one requirement in your own DataMan work.

Your artifact

**This is practice, not another submission.** Nothing from this lab is submitted separately. Use the feedback to improve the story work that will later appear in `docs/product-backlog.md`.

[Open Story Builder Lab](https://amlw05.github.io/cts-285-course-simulations/m3/story-builder/)

Analyst rule

**When you finish.** Carry one improved story idea into your own product backlog work. Keep the requirement ID visible so the story remains traceable.
