MODULE 4 · USE THE EVIDENCE

## M4.2 — Investigate Your Product Slice

You have completed the controlled labs and submitted your **M4 Lab Evidence Record**. Now use that evidence to return to the product slice you selected earlier in the module.

**The job changes here.** The labs gave you known problems to operate and revise. The Design Studio asks you to decide what evidence is needed for *your* product work and use that evidence to make a defensible build-readiness decision.

### Bring these with you

- `docs/design/m4-investigation-brief.md`;
- your completed M4 Lab Evidence Record;
- the relevant Module 2 requirements and traceability;
- your Module 3 backlog and acceptance criteria;
- the revised or new design question that emerged from the labs.

### Do not build artifacts by habit

Your question should determine the evidence you create. You might need a behavior flow, wireframe, ERD fragment, field/state table, sequence sketch, dependency check, acceptance-criteria trace, structural spike, or another representation that directly tests the uncertainty.

**You are not required to make all of these.** Use the minimum evidence needed to answer the question and support the decision.

### DataMan Design Studio

Open the Studio in a new tab. Use your own selected product slice, test the question you brought forward, and revise when the evidence exposes a problem or changes your reasoning.

[Open the DataMan Design Studio](https://amlw05.github.io/cts-285-course-simulations/m4/design-studio/)

### Leave the Studio with a decision

Your investigation should give you enough evidence to decide whether the selected work should:

**proceed · revise · split · gather more evidence · block/return for clarification · defer**

**When you return to Canvas:** you will turn the Studio evidence into a formal Design Investigation Application. That graded record will show the question you investigated, the evidence you used, what changed, and why your final disposition is defensible.

**Nothing is submitted on this page.** Complete the Studio, return to this Canvas tab, and use **Next** to continue.
