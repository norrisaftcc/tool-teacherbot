## M4 Lab Evidence Record

You have now tested two controlled DataMan design problems. This checkpoint captures the evidence that should influence your next design decision.

**Submit evidence of testing and revision, not a summary of what the labs contained.** Your record should show what you tested, what consequence became visible, what you changed, and what happened after the revision.

### Build your evidence record

Open the Lab Evidence Record generator in a new tab. Complete the required fields for both labs and the cross-lab finding. The download button will unlock only after the required evidence is present.

[Open the M4 Lab Evidence Record](https://amlw05.github.io/cts-285-course-simulations/m4/lab-evidence-record/)

### Your record must include

- **Answer Flow Lab:** the design decision tested, the scenario that exposed the issue, the consequence observed, the revision made, and what changed afterward.
- **Memory Bank & Curator Retrieval Lab:** the design decision tested, the scenario that exposed the issue, the consequence observed, the revision made, and what changed afterward.
- **Cross-lab finding:** what became visible through operating the systems that was not obvious from simply reading the requirements.
- **Next design question:** the question that should now guide your own DataMan investigation.

**Why this matters:** systems work produces decisions that must be traceable to evidence. This record becomes part of the reasoning you will use when you investigate your own product slice and decide whether it is ready to build.

### Submit in Canvas

1. Generate the completed record.
2. Download `m4-lab-evidence-record.md`.
3. Upload that file to this assignment.

**Do not submit screenshots of the labs.** The evidence record is the submission.

### Grading · 6 points

This checkpoint uses two 3-point criteria:

- **Evidence from Controlled Testing** — specific, credible evidence from both labs.
- **Revision and Transfer** — revisions follow from evidence and the cross-lab finding produces a useful next design question.
