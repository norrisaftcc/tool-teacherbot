MODULE 4 · PRACTICE THE JUDGMENT

## Before You Design: What Would You Ask First?

In **Design for Implementation**, you examined the shift from product work to implementation-supporting design. Now you will practice the judgment that makes that shift useful: deciding **what question should be answered next, what evidence could answer it, and what should happen when the evidence changes the situation.**

**The goal is not to guess a preferred answer.** Systems analysts and developers often work with incomplete information. The professional skill is recognizing which uncertainty matters, selecting evidence that can expose the problem, and deciding what the team should do next.

### Practice before the investigation

The **Design Decisions Cabinet** gives you several short situations involving design questions, layer responsibility, data relationships, and build readiness.

As you work, pay attention to three things:

- **Question:** What does the team actually need to know before choosing a solution?
- **Evidence:** What could show whether the design works, fails, or creates a new problem?
- **Decision:** What should the team do next based on that evidence?
