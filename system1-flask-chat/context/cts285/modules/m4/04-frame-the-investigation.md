MODULE 4 · FRAME THE INVESTIGATION

## From Practice to Investigation: Frame the Question

The Design Decisions Cabinet gave you short, controlled situations. Now move from practice into actual DataMan product work. Choose one product slice from your Module 3 backlog and identify **what still needs to be understood before implementation should begin.**

**Think like a systems analyst:** do not begin by choosing a diagram, screen, database, or code structure. Begin by naming the uncertainty that could create rework, ambiguity, or a weak implementation decision.

### Bring forward your existing work

- your Module 3 backlog and acceptance criteria;
- the relevant Module 2 requirements and traceability;
- DataMan source evidence connected to the product slice;
- any assumption or unresolved question already recorded.

### Your investigation starts with one product slice

Select one story or a small connected set of stories. Then use the Investigation Brief to trace the slice, identify one uncertainty, write the question the team needs answered, choose one or two forms of evidence, and explain why that evidence is a reasonable starting point.

### DataMan Design Investigation Brief

Open the investigation environment in a new tab. Complete the brief and copy the generated Markdown into your CTS-285 repository at `docs/design/m4-investigation-brief.md`.

[Open the DataMan Design Investigation Brief](https://amlw05.github.io/cts-285-course-simulations/m4/design-investigation/)

### Do not solve the design yet

The brief is a starting position. Your question may change when later testing exposes stronger evidence or a more important uncertainty.

**When you return to Canvas:** make sure `m4-investigation-brief.md` is saved in your repository. Nothing is submitted in Canvas yet. Keep the brief available for the next simulation.

Return to this Canvas tab when your brief is saved, then use **Next** to continue.
