CTS-285 · Module 8 Handoff

## Design Brief + Design-Consult Record

The capstone handoff must communicate the pod's capstone direction to someone who did not build the system. Your existing wireframes show structure. The design brief explains audience, constraints, terminology, and what kind of design work the project will need.

### The design brief is a promise, not a report

Write it from the evidence the handoff currently supports. Do not describe features the team hopes might exist someday as if they are committed scope.

Create `docs/capstone/design-brief.md` with these sections:

Section | What it communicates

**Direction / positioning** | the problem space, intended audience, and current direction without pretending a final product choice is already settled;

**Primary audience** | a specific user situation rather than a vague demographic;

**Terminology** | the labels and domain language a designer should preserve or question;

**Design evidence** | links to the validated wireframes or interaction sketches;

**Technical/product constraints** | constraints described in language that changes a design decision, not framework names;

**Requested deliverables** | what design help/assets are needed, where they will be used, and any known size/format constraints;

**Accessibility constraints** | requirements such as readable contrast and not using color as the only signal.

### The consult is evidence of exchange

Create `docs/capstone/design-consult-record.md`. Record:

- what you shared and when;
- questions, critique, or direction that came back;
- what changed in the handoff as a result;
- one critique you declined or deferred, with a reason, when applicable;
- the design contact or next-contact status if one exists.

**External dependency rule:** the consult is an input, not a condition another person must satisfy for you to earn credit. You are evaluated on what you prepared, what feedback was available, and how you responded.

### Get the required critique in this course

The required critique is course-controlled. Bring your design brief and wireframes to the scheduled M8 design-review/studio session. Your instructor will critique the handoff while pods rotate through review.

1. **Prepare the brief and evidence.** Bring the same material you would give to an outside design contact.
2. **Record the instructor critique.** Capture the questions, concerns, or direction that affect the handoff.
3. **Respond to it.** Record what changed and any critique you declined or deferred, with a reason.
4. **Add external feedback if it arrives.** A GRD/external consult may strengthen the record, but it is additional evidence and never a condition for completion.

How this is graded

You are graded on the brief you prepared, the critique you obtained, and how you responded to it. An outside contact may add evidence, but another person's response time can never reduce your grade.

### What not to do

- do not make polished visual mockups merely to make the handoff look finished;
- do not request vague assets such as “a logo” without placement/context;
- do not describe technical constraints only with framework/database names;
- do not describe optional external feedback as required for completion;
- do not let a pending external response block completion of your own handoff evidence.
