## Product Handoff Decision

You are not choosing and fully defining the capstone product here. You are making the decisions this course can support honestly so the next course receives a direction instead of an empty field.

Analyst rule

**A signposted decision is a deliverable, not a gap.** An unresolved choice is acceptable when you name it, explain why it remains open, and identify what evidence or action should settle it.

### Decide the capstone direction

In the **Product handoff status** section of your handoff manifest, record four things:

1. **Problem space:** what kind of problem the pod wants the capstone to address and why that direction is worth pursuing.
2. **Stack starting point:** the technologies you would begin from based on what you know now. Treat this as a starting point, not a permanent requirement.
3. **Scope ambition:** what the pod believes could reasonably be completed during a sixteen-week capstone.
4. **Deliberately open decisions:** choices you are not ready to settle yet, with a reason and the evidence or action that should settle each one.

### Use evidence you already have

Your direction should grow from the work already completed in CTS-285: what your pod found useful, difficult, interesting, reusable, risky, or unfinished. Do not rewrite the DataMan backlog as if it belongs to the new capstone, and do not invent a complete product simply to make the handoff look finished.

**DataMan stops here as the shared practice project.** What carries forward is the evidence of how you analyze, design, test, review, make tradeoffs, and document decisions. The stack you used here is a starting point, not a requirement for the capstone.

### What not to do

- do not choose a product from nothing just to fill a blank;
- do not present a tentative technology choice as permanent;
- do not silently rewrite old DataMan evidence into capstone evidence;
- do not hide unresolved decisions;
- do not create another assignment or file when the handoff manifest already has a place for this evidence.

How this is graded

This page is required and formative. It carries no separate points. Your attributable handoff decisions may be evaluated through the existing **Capstone Handoff Package**, which is already the graded M8 evidence.
