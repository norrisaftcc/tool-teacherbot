CTS-285 · Course Wrap-Up

## What CTS-285 Hands Forward

CTS-285 ends with a handoff, not a reset. What you carry into the capstone is the practice: how you reason from evidence, how you decide, and how you record what you decided. Your pod, your working agreement, your capstone direction, your risks, and your planning records go forward with you.

DataMan does not. It is the practice project for this course, built so that every student learns the same behaviour of a database-driven website. Your requirements, backlog, design evidence, application, and tests are the proof that you can do the work. They are worked examples, not the capstone's starting material.

Python, Flask and SQLite were a teaching choice for the same reason: one stack, so that everyone met the same problems. **The next course lets your team choose its own stack.** Treat what you used here as a starting point you can defend, not a requirement you inherit.

### The course progression

**Evidence → need → requirement → product work → design → implementation → executable verification → review → iteration → capstone handoff.**

### What should now be visible in your work

- decisions that point back to evidence rather than preference;
- requirements and acceptance criteria another person can evaluate;
- design and implementation records that survive module boundaries;
- tests that can disagree with the code and trigger investigation;
- reviews that distinguish defects from decisions and gaps;
- two sprint cycles with individually attributable role evidence;
- risks and unknowns named before they become somebody else's surprise;
- a package the receiving course can inspect instead of reconstruct.

**The handoff is allowed to contain unknowns.** Professional evidence is not evidence that everything is settled. It is evidence that what is settled, what is not, and why are visible to the people who inherit the work.

### What the next course should not have to make you redo

Do not treat validated evidence as disposable coursework once its grade is recorded. The handoff manifest identifies what should be reused, what needs verification, and what remains intentionally open.

Analyst rule

Leave the repository in a state where the receiving course can begin from the handoff manifest, follow the evidence paths, and see the decisions and unknowns without depending on your memory of CTS-285.
