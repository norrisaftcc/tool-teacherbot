## M1.1 — What Is Systems Analysis & Design?

Systems analysis and design is the work of understanding a real need and turning that understanding into a solution a team can build responsibly.

Analyst rule

**Analysis asks what problem and need the system must address. Design asks how the solution should work.**

### One discipline, two connected questions

#### Systems analysis

Investigates the current situation, users, evidence, problems, opportunities, and requirements. Its job is to establish **what the system should accomplish and why**.

#### Systems design

Turns validated needs and requirements into decisions about interfaces, behavior, data, responsibilities, and technical structure. Its job is to establish **how the solution will work**.

### What makes this different from simply programming?

Programming produces working software. Systems analysis and design helps determine whether the software being built is the right software, whether its behavior can be justified, and whether its structure supports the problem the organization actually needs solved.

### A simple project lens

**Need:** What is happening now, and why does it matter?

**Requirement:** What must the future system do or satisfy?

**Design:** How should the solution behave and be structured?

**Implementation:** How do we build and verify the chosen design?

### Where CTS-285 is headed

DataMan will give you one continuing project for practicing this discipline. You will investigate evidence, write requirements, turn those requirements into product work, make design decisions, build an implementation, test it, review it, and carry the resulting evidence forward.

Outcome

Explain the purpose of systems analysis and design and distinguish the question analysis answers from the question design answers.

### Quick self-check

****A stakeholder says, “Add a dashboard.” Is that automatically a requirement?****

No. It is a proposed solution. Analysis first asks what need the dashboard is supposed to address and what evidence supports that need.

****If the requirement is already validated, what question comes next?****

How the solution should work well enough for a team to build, test, and evaluate it.
